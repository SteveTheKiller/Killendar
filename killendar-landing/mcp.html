Killendar MCP

Ask your agent what is on your calendar.

What it does

KillerMCP gives your agent read-only access to your Killendar agenda, including recurring events. Choose a start date and a range of up to 31 days.

killer what is on my calendar this week

The tool reads your local calendar without changing appointments. Encrypted calendars cannot currently be unlocked through MCP.

One connection for the family

Killendar is part of KillerMCP, the shared connection for KillerTools and the Killer apps. You install KillerMCP once. It finds supported apps on your computer and makes their tools available to your agent.

The installer and Killendar integration are still in development. Setup instructions and a download link will appear here when they are ready for release.
